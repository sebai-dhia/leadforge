Find local businesses that need you, and a way to reach them.

Describe your service, pick a city or draw an area on the map, and review a ranked list of businesses with phone, email and social links. Leadforge drafts the message. You press send.

From a service idea to a message in three steps.

  1. Tell us what you offer

    Pick your services. Leadforge suggests the kinds of businesses most likely to need them.

  2. Pick where to look

    Type up to three cities, or draw an area on the map. Results come with phone, email and social links when they exist.

  3. Review, then reach out

    Reachable leads rank first. Open a draft in English, French or Arabic and send it by email or WhatsApp yourself.
